# Virunga Ecotours

Virunga Ecotours is a premier travel agency based in Rwanda, specializing in authentic community tourism, gorilla trekking, and immersive cultural experiences across Rwanda, Uganda, and the DR Congo.

## Core Services
- **Wildlife Encounters**: Gorilla trekking, birdwatching, and safaris.
- **Cultural Discovery**: Museum visits and community-based tourism.
- **Adventure Collection**: Cycling and mountain trekking.
- **Travel Assurance**: Car hire, safety support, and evacuation services.

## Travel Styles
- **Luxury**: High-end immersive journeys.
- **Mid-Range**: Signature Virunga journeys.
- **Budget**: Essential explorer journeys.

## Key Destinations
- **Rwanda**: Volcanoes National Park, Musanze, and Kigali.
- **Uganda**: Bwindi Impenetrable Forest and Mgahinga.
- **DR Congo**: Virunga Massif.
