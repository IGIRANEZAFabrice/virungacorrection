## Wildlife Encounters: Mountain Gorillas
Explore the legendary gorilla families of the Virunga Massif. From the iconic Susa family to the rising dynasties of Igisha, Isimbi, and more.

### Iconic Families
- **Susa Family**: The legend from which Igisha, Isimbi, and Karisimbi were born.
- **Kwitonda Family**: Wisdom and resilience, arriving from DRC in 2005.
- **Pablo Family**: A dynasty that once reached 65 members.
- **Amahoro Family**: Known for its peaceful nature.
